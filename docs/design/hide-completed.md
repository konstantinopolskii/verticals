# Hide completed goals

A board where everything done stays in sight gets long, and the open work sinks under it. One setting takes the done goals
off the board, and the same setting brings them back.

## Behaviour

- **Where:** View → Hide Completed Goals in the desktop app, a checkmark with ⇧⌘H, like Show Completed in Reminders. The
  same keys work in a browser (Ctrl+Shift+H elsewhere), and the keyboard guide lists them. The choice is kept on this device.
- **What hides:** every done goal in the board's columns, the Inbox's and the moving board's included, and the steps
  under a goal too. A done goal with open steps leaves them on the board, each a row of its own.
- **Checking one off:** a goal keeps its place while its check draws, then leaves, and the rows below glide up. With
  motion reduced it leaves at once. Undo brings it back where it was.
- **Still there:** a goal's own window and search show done goals, and progress ("2/5") still counts them.

## Done when

The board leaves done goals out with the setting on and shows them with it off, nested steps included; a goal checked
off leaves after its check; the choice survives a reload; ⇧⌘H switches it and the guide lists it; the desktop app's item
switches it and its tick follows the page.
