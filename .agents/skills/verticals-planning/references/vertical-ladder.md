# Verticals ladder

Classify a card by the commitment it makes, not by how long it may take.

| Level | Verb | Commitment | Test | Done means |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Life value | become | An enduring identity or life direction. | Would this still matter in an obituary? | Lived and retained as history; not ordinary task completion. |
| Life condition | secure | A durable state or threshold required by a value. | Would sustaining it materially enable the value? | Reached and maintained, or explicitly redefined. |
| Principle | practice | A repeated pursuit that compounds into character. | Would years of practice change who the person becomes? | Absorbed into identity or consciously released. |
| 3 years | visualise | A visible, falsifiable future state. | Can the state be observed and proven wrong? | Picture landed or was explicitly repainted. |
| Year | bet | A chosen portfolio direction. | If it fails, was it still a rational bet? | Bet resolved; snapshot retained. |
| Quarter | achieve | A measurable outcome or artifact. | What exists or changed at quarter end? | Outcome achieved or miss reviewed. |
| Month | plan | A sequenced route toward an outcome. | In what order and by what route? | Route executed through its weeks. |
| Week | execute | A bounded set of committed shots. | What will be completed this week? | Actual shots recorded and reviewed. |
| Day | focus | One shot receiving attention now. | What is the thing now? | Shot fired or explicitly dropped. |

## Placement rules

1. Use one strongest causal parent.
2. Every non-value active goal must resolve to exactly one parentless Life value.
3. A Life subgoal is a condition or principle, not another value root.
4. A 3-year card is a future picture, not a distant task list.
5. A Year card is a strategic bet; measurable promised results normally belong at Quarter or lower.
6. Month is route, Week is committed execution, Day is current attention. They are not generic buckets.
7. A missing rung is design debt only when it breaks causality. Propose it; do not invent it.
8. Root order can carry strategy. Preserve it unless reordering is explicitly approved.

Ideas, observations, unsupported tasks, and orphans do not belong in the active ladder. Preserve useful evidence and propose a precise disposition instead of silently deleting history.
