# Carry-over goals

KK's 26 September 2026 ruling overrides R10's carried placement and D125's earlier
visual treatment: carried goals now follow the planned section and its add field. D103's
faded ghost treatment is replaced by full-strength titles plus the original-period suffix.
R10's live-period rule (16 August), D168 and D230 still hold: carried goals remain projections
of their stored schedules, never silent reschedules; historical periods do not carry them.
Current-period goals (open, then done) and their add field sit above a thin red now-line.
Carried families sit below it, full strength, newest original anchor first. The line count
includes every rendered carried descendant. Each title ends with its own original period
in readable grey; there is no per-card Due mark. Same-vertical families remain nested at every
depth (R7/D253). Historical columns read their own canonical card metadata and do not inherit
another column's ghost decoration. `carryover-ghost.css` preserves full opacity.
